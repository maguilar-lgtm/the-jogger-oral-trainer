La historia por etapas

1. Dan and Sue are police officers in London. Dan is angry because someone steals money from people near the shops every day.
2. They watch the street because a jogger runs near the shops every day and sometimes runs into people.
3. The jogger runs into an old woman. She falls down. He stops and helps her, but another young woman shouts at him.
4. A police car stops the jogger in North Street. His name is Peter Jones. He says he likes running and he never takes money.
5. The old woman says her money was in her purse, but the purse is missing. Dan and Sue are not sure Peter is the thief.
6. They return to the shops and find a TV camera over a shop door. They watch the video.
7. The video shows the old woman taking money from the bank, putting it in her purse and then in her bag.
8. The jogger runs into her, stops and helps her. Dan and Sue also notice a long-haired man and another woman near the old lady.
9. Dan cannot clearly see what happens to the bag. He makes a plan: the next morning they will take money from the bank and watch what happens.
Importante: no se inventa el final. Este entrenador llega hasta la página 10, que es lo que aparece en las fotos.

Resumen corto para memorizar

Dan and Sue are police officers in London.
They are trying to find someone who steals money near the shops.
At first, they suspect a jogger called Peter Jones.
After watching a TV camera recording, they realize other people may be involved.
Dan makes a plan to catch the thief the next morning.

Conectores útiles

At first... - Al principio
Then... - Luego
After that... - Después
However... - Sin embargo
Finally, in this part of the story...
